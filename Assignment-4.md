Differentiate between Normal Distribution and Binomial Distribution.

**Normal Distribution**                                                                     **Binomial Distribution**

1\. It is a continuous probability distribution.                                            1\. It is a discrete probability distribution.

2\. It has a bell-shaped curve.                                                             2\. It is usually represented using bars.

3\. It is symmetrical around the mean.                                                      3\. It may be symmetrical or skewed.

4\. It can take any value within a given range.                                             4\. It takes whole-number values (0, 1, 2, 3, etc.).

5\. It is described by mean and standard deviation.                                         5\. It is described by number of trials (n) and probability of success (p).

6\. It is used for continuous data.                                                         6\. It is used for counting the number of successes.

7\. Examples include height, weight, and temperature.                                       7\. Examples include number of heads in coin tosses.

8\. Its shape is generally bell-shaped.                                                     8\. Its shape depends on the number of trials and probability of success.
